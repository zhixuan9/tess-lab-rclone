Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

tess-lab-rclone is used only by its owner, to sync files between the owner's server and the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive folders that the owner chooses.
  • It collects no data from anyone else.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's server.
  • The owner can remove access at any time in their Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.
